Solid button labels, muted text and invalid-field borders meet WCAG AA contrast in the light, mid and dark themes, with the stock brand and with a `createTheme()` brand.

**Visible changes to check in your app:** `AlertDialogAction` (and `ConfirmDialog`'s regular confirm) has a white label in every theme; it was gray in the mid theme and near-black in dark. Solid brand and destructive buttons get darker on hover and press instead of lighter in light mode: the `brand` `Button`, `AlertDialogAction`, a selected `Calendar` day and `ConfirmDialog`'s `destructive` confirm (brand `#ae00d9` hovered and `#9a00c1` pressed, red `#c62222` and `#b01e1e`; in mid and dark they were already darker and are now the same colours). Invalid fields have a different red border: darker in light mode (`#b91c1c`, was `#dc2626`), a light red in dark (`#fca5a5`) and a pale red in mid (`#fee2e2`); that is every `Field`-aware text control, `Checkbox`, `Switch` and `InputOTP`. Muted text is slightly lighter in dark mode (`#adadad`, was `#a3a3a3`). Check marks and labels on the brand colour are white in mid and dark (they were the page colour): `Checkbox`, `TreeView`, `MultiCombobox` and `RadioCard` marks, a complete `Steps` circle, the toast action button, the `AppAccountMenu` / `AppMobileNav` avatar initials and the selected `Combobox` option. On that option the description is solid white (was 70%) and the avatar chip is a darker brand tint (was a lighter one), in light mode too. In the mid theme a pressed `primary` `Button` keeps its hover fill. With `createTheme()`, the label on the brand colour can change: black on brands such as `#924ff7` (it was taupe in light mode and white in dark), and the dark label of a light brand now also applies in dark mode (it was white there).

- **`AlertDialogAction` label:** `bg-primary text-primary-foreground`, the solid `brand` `Button`'s pair, instead of `bg-brand-primary text-background`. The label was 1.3:1 in the mid theme and 3.9:1 in dark; it is 4.6:1 in every theme. The same swap (`text-primary-foreground` for `text-background`) on the other brand fills listed above.
- **Hover and pressed fills mix toward a shade instead of fading.** A fill at 90% / 80% alpha takes the colour behind it, so over a white page it got lighter under its white label: 4.3:1 and 3.9:1 on the brand, 4.3:1 and 3.8:1 on the destructive confirm. New Tailwind colours `primary-hover`, `primary-active`, `destructive-hover` and `destructive-active` (`bg-primary-hover`, …) mix 10% / 20% of a shade into the fill: 5.5:1 and 6.7:1 on the brand, 5.8:1 and 6.9:1 on destructive, the same in light, mid and dark. They are `color-mix()` expressions over `--primary` / `--destructive`, so they follow an overridden fill. The brand mixes toward the new `--primary-shade` token (black; `createTheme()` sets white for a brand with a dark label, so the fill always moves away from the label), destructive toward black. Use them for your own solid buttons in place of `hover:bg-primary/90`.
- **`primary` `Button` in the mid theme:** the pressed fill (`foreground` at 80%) was 4.1–4.4:1 under the gray label; in mid it stays at the hover fill (4.9–5.0:1). Light and dark are unchanged.
- **Dark `--muted-foreground` is `#adadad`** (was ground-400 `#a3a3a3`): 4.6:1 on `surface-8` and on `accent` (was 4.1:1), where a dialog opened from a dialog puts its description, and 5.1:1 on `surface-7` (was 4.5:1). It stays 2.1:1 dimmer than the regular text. Light and mid already passed on every surface level and are unchanged.
- **Invalid borders use `--destructive-text`.** `--destructive` as a border was 1.2–1.9:1 against the mid surfaces and 2.2–2.9:1 on raised dark ones (surface-4 and up). The error edge is now at least 6.2:1 in light, 4.9:1 in mid and 5.5:1 in dark on every surface: `Input`, `Textarea`, `Select`, `NativeSelect`, `Combobox`, `DatePicker`, `InputNumber`, `TimePicker`, `ColorInput`, `MultiCombobox`, `TagInput`, `InputGroup`, `InputOTP`, and the `Checkbox` / `Switch` error outline. The focus halo keeps the destructive colour (`ring-destructive/35`).
- **`createTheme()` brand label:** `--primary-foreground` is the first of white, taupe and black that is 4.5:1 on the brand, and it is emitted for light, dark and mid alike. Before, a brand between luminance 0.18 and 0.23 got taupe below 4.5:1 (`#924ff7`: 3.8:1), and dark mode always got white (4.48:1 on `#924ff7`, 1.6:1 on a yellow brand). `#924ff7` now takes black (4.7:1); one step darker (`#914ef7`) takes white (4.5:1). The `[data-theme="mid"]` block now carries `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--primary-shade` and `--ring` as well, so a mid subtree inside a light or dark page uses the brand instead of the stock purple. The result has two new fields, `primaryForeground` and `primaryShade`. If you set `--primary` by hand with a dark `--primary-foreground`, set `--primary-shade: #ffffff` too.
